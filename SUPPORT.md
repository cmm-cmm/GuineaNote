# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Press F1 in the editor for the keyboard guide and F2 for
  every action with its key. The [shortcut reference](docs/KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md),
  [editor behavior](docs/EDITOR_BEHAVIOR.md) and
  [terminal workflow](docs/TERMINAL_WORKFLOW.md) cover the details.
- **Something is broken:** search the
  [issues](https://github.com/cmm-cmm/GuineaNote/issues), then open a bug
  report with `guinea --version`, the OS, the terminal and the exact keys.
- **An idea:** open a feature request and describe the workflow it helps.
- **A vulnerability:** report it privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Guinea Note is maintained in spare time; answers may take a few days. Issues
in English or Vietnamese are both fine.
